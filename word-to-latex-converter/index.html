Conversion Workflow & Capabilities

1

Document Ingestion

Drag and drop or select your .docx file. The browser creates an in-memory ArrayBuffer directly from your file handle without uploading a single byte.

2

WebAssembly Translation

Pandoc 3.10 WebAssembly decompresses in a sandboxed Web Worker thread, parses OpenXML structures, translates equations to LaTeX syntax, and unwraps container elements.

3

Clipboard & Export

Copy the resulting LaTeX fragment to your clipboard with one click, or download a full .tex document ready for compilation in Overleaf or TeXstudio.

Honest Capabilities & Document Scope

The converter uses the battle-tested Pandoc document parser. While it handles typical scholarly papers, reports, lists, headings, tables, and standard equations with high fidelity, no automated converter translates every arbitrary Word layout into perfect LaTeX.

  • Supported: Heading hierarchies (H1–H6), paragraph styling, bold/italics, bulleted and numbered lists, footnotes, inline and block math, and standard tables.
  • Cleaned: Automatically strips noisy Pandoc artifacts like \tightlist and unwraps \pandocbounded{} wrappers for clean TeX editing.
  • Manual Review Advised: Heavily nested multi-column layouts, custom Word SmartArt drawings, complex vector diagrams, and legacy Word 97-2003 equations (Equation 3.0) may require manual adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my Word documents or manuscripts uploaded to a remote server?

No. The converter runs Pandoc 3.10 compiled to WebAssembly inside a dedicated Web Worker thread in your browser. Document parsing, XML extraction, and LaTeX generation take place directly on your device. Document bytes are not uploaded or transmitted over the network.

How does this online converter differ from the Word to LaTeX browser extension?

The online converter runs on this webpage and handles DOCX file conversions through your web browser. The installable Word to LaTeX (Offline) Microsoft Edge extension bundles the entire engine offline, integrates directly into your browser toolbar, supports clipboard table conversions, includes experimental local OCR, and works offline without needing to visit a website.

What Word formatting elements are preserved during conversion?

The converter preserves heading levels (H1-H6), bold and italic typography, bulleted and numbered lists, text alignments, inline math, display equations, and data tables formatted as clean booktabs or longtable LaTeX environments.

What are the limitations of the converter?

The converter is optimized for standard scholarly, technical, and business DOCX documents. Unusually complex nested drawing canvas shapes, non-standard equation objects from legacy Word versions, custom macros, or deeply embedded OLE objects may require minor manual adjustment in your LaTeX editor.

Can I use the generated LaTeX code in Overleaf or TeXstudio?

Yes. In LaTeX Fragment mode, the output provides clean body code ready to paste directly into existing Overleaf or TeXstudio documents. In Complete Document mode, it includes a compilable LaTeX preamble with documentclass and standard packages.
