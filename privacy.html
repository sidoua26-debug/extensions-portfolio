Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Data Controller: sidoua26
Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero remote servers, everything local. We do not track you, collect personal data, or transmit your files over the network.

This privacy policy explains our commitment to transparency regarding this portfolio website (the "Site") and provides a factual, plain-language breakdown of how each published browser extension handles user data.

1. The Portfolio Website

This portfolio is a 100% static client-side website hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Our server-side footprint is zero.

  • No Cookies or Tracking: The Site does not set any cookies, advertising beacons, or tracking identifiers.
  • No Analytics: There are no third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or tracking pixels) running on the Site.
  • Local Storage (Theme Preference): The Site uses your browser's localStorage solely to save your choice of Light or Dark mode (theme). This data is stored strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • 100% Self-Hosted Assets: All stylesheets, fonts (Geist and JetBrains Mono WOFF2), icons, images, and scripts are 100% self-hosted on the same origin. Zero external CDNs, zero third-party font requests, and zero remote tracking scripts.
  • External Store Links: Clicking store links redirects you to third-party marketplaces (Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Chrome Web Store) or GitHub, which maintain their own independent privacy policies.

2. Browser Extensions Data Handling

Every extension listed in this portfolio is engineered with privacy as an architectural constraint: no telemetry, no analytics, no remote servers, and everything local.

Word to LaTeX (Offline)

100% Offline · Zero Network Access. Word to LaTeX converts Microsoft Word (.docx) files, equations, and tables entirely inside your local browser memory using WebAssembly (Pandoc WASM) and local OCR (Tesseract.js). Manifest V3 requests permissions: []—it does not possess permission to initiate network requests. Your manuscripts, research documents, and clipboard contents never leave your device.

Table Extractor

100% Local In-Tab Processing. Table Extractor extracts HTML tables and virtualized data grids directly within your browser's active tab. Data parsing, column filtering, and export generation (CSV, XLSX with UTF-8 BOM, JSON) run strictly in local memory. Manifest V3 uses only activeTab and scripting on explicit user interaction. Zero table data is ever transmitted to any remote server.

BulkGrab

100% Local Download & Bundling. BulkGrab discovers downloadable links and images on the page you are viewing. Downloads stream directly from the source host website into your browser's native download folder without proxying through any intermediary server. ZIP archive bundling is performed locally on-device via JSZip. No download URLs, filenames, or content metadata are ever logged or uploaded.

SEO Inspector

100% In-Browser Diagnostics. SEO Inspector audits the active webpage's DOM, meta tags, heading hierarchies, Open Graph previews, and Navigation Timing metrics entirely client-side. Browsing history is never stored, tracked, or transmitted. Manifest V3 requests only activeTab and scripting upon user request. Zero analytics or diagnostic telemetry are sent to external servers.

Bilexi

100% Offline Autocorrect & Accent Restoration. Bilexi runs entirely offline within your local browser sandbox. All dictionary lookups, bilingual English/French spell checking, and French accent restorations (e.g. ecole → école) execute in local memory. Keystrokes and text inputs are never transmitted over any network, logged to disk, or sent to cloud APIs. Custom vocabulary and user preferences reside exclusively on your local device via chrome.storage.local.
