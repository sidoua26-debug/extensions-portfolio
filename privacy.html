Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Data Controller: sidoua26

This privacy policy explains our commitment to transparency regarding this portfolio website (the "Site") and provides a factual overview of how our browser extensions process user data.

1. The Portfolio Website

This portfolio is a 100% static client-side website designed to showcase browser extensions. Our server-side footprint is zero.

  • No Cookies or Tracking: The Site does not set any cookies, advertising beacons, or tracking identifiers.
  • No Analytics: There are no third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics or tracking pixels) running on the Site.
  • Local Storage (Theme Preference): The Site uses your browser's localStorage solely to save your choice of Light or Dark mode (theme). This data is stored strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • External Fonts: The typography is provided via Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When you load the Site, your browser requests font files directly from Google's content delivery network according to Google's standard privacy policies.
  • External Links: Clicking store links redirects you to third-party marketplaces (Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Chrome Web Store) or GitHub, which have their own respective privacy policies.

2. Browser Extensions Data Handling

Every extension listed in this portfolio is designed to respect your privacy with minimal permissions and clear data boundaries. Below is an accurate breakdown by product:

Word to LaTeX (Offline)

Runs fully offline, nothing leaves your device. All document parsing (Pandoc WebAssembly) and optical character recognition (Tesseract.js) execute entirely inside your local browser sandbox without network communication. Requests permissions: [] in its Manifest V3 definition.

Table Extractor

Your table data stays in your browser; nothing is sent to any server. Scans and processes HTML tables and virtualized data grids locally in your active tab upon explicit user interaction (using activeTab and scripting).

BulkGrab

Files download directly from the source site to your device and are bundled locally; no upload to my servers. Downloads are managed directly by your browser's native download manager without proxying files through any remote server.

SEO Inspector

Analyzes the page in your browser; no data sent to any server. Evaluates page DOM structure, meta tags, schema markup, and performance timing in-memory on the active tab without logging or transmitting browsing history.

Bilexi

Runs 100% offline with zero cloud telemetry. All dictionary lookups, accent restoration, and language models execute completely within your browser's local sandbox. Keystrokes and text inputs are processed in-memory and are never stored or transmitted over any network. Preferences and custom vocabulary are stored strictly on your local device via chrome.storage.local.
