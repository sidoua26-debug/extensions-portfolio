Why Convert Word to LaTeX Offline?

Most online DOCX-to-LaTeX conversion services require uploading your unpublished academic manuscripts, proprietary corporate documents, and private math formulas to third-party cloud servers. These servers can log, retain, or index your content without your knowledge.

Word to LaTeX (Offline) eliminates cloud risk by compiling the industry-standard Pandoc document engine directly into WebAssembly. When you drag and drop a .docx file into the extension, conversion happens inside your local browser memory in an isolated Web Worker thread.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Word to LaTeX convert documents without an internet connection?

The extension bundles a compiled WebAssembly binary of Pandoc. All parsing of DOCX XML structures, mathematical formulas, and tables executes within a dedicated local Web Worker thread inside your browser. No files are ever uploaded.

How does the extension compare to the online web converter?

The installed extension provides persistent toolbar access, clipboard table paste, and completely offline functionality without loading web assets. If you prefer not to install an extension, our free online converter offers the same in-browser WebAssembly conversion directly on our website.

Does the extension preserve equations and table formatting?

Yes. Word to LaTeX preserves inline and display equations, formats tables using clean booktabs or longtable syntax, and maintains document headings, bold/italics, and nested lists.

What are the output mode options?

You can generate a LaTeX Fragment (body content only, ideal for pasting into an existing Overleaf paper or book chapter) or a Complete Document (full standalone document with \documentclass and required package imports).

Is Word to LaTeX free to use?

Yes, Word to LaTeX is 100% free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no account requirements. The source code is publicly available on GitHub for transparency and inspection, while third-party components are governed by their respective open-source licenses.
